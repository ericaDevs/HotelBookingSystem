The Genesis

A Vision Rooted in Authentic Kenyan Warmth

Inter-county Gardens Hotel Kitui originated from a bold ambition: to deliver an oasis of five-star tranquility and refined service amidst the breathtaking landscapes of Kitui County. Inspired by the legendary graciousness of Akamba culture, our founders set out to replace standard transit accommodations with a vibrant lifestyle destination.

Over the years, we have grown into the cornerstone of civic and corporate gatherings in the county. Whether hosting diplomatic delegations, high-profile symposia, or families reuniting for memorable weekend retreats beneath the sun, our gates offer an elevated home where heritage seamlessly intersects with modern opulence.
