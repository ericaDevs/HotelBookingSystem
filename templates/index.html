A Tranquil Haven Where Kitui Culture Meets Uncompromised Luxury

Nestled along Kalawa Road against the gentle breezes of Kitui County, Inter-county Gardens Hotel offers retreat for leisure wanderers, high-level business summits, and weekend getaways. Our hotel pays homage to authentic Kenyan cordiality, centered around shimmering waters, flowering acacia canopies, and architectural finesse.

Whether unwinding with a chilled hibiscus tea across our signature arched pool bridge, delighting in aromatic Swahili delicacies, or hosting corporate summits in tech-enabled halls, experience world-class pampering right in the heart of eastern Kenya.
