Helpful answers regarding reservations, logistics, and guest facilities at Intercounty Hotel Kitui.

Yes, we arrange private chauffeur transfers to and from the Kitui Airstrip (10 minutes away) as well as executive pickups from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (JKIA) Nairobi upon advance booking. Please notify our reservations desk at least 24 hours prior to arrival.
We accept direct Safaricom M-Pesa Buy Goods & Services till payments, major international credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), and direct corporate bank wire transfers. Official electronic VAT receipts are provided immediately.
Standard check-in begins at 12:00 PM (Noon) and check-out is at 10:00 AM. Early check-in or delayed check-out can be arranged subject to room availability; kindly coordinate with the front desk in advance.
Intercounty Hotel features four multipurpose conference venues ranging from intimate 20-seat executive boardrooms to our grand ballroom catering for up to 350 delegates theater-style. All suites are equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, modern projection, PA systems, and bespoke tea-break catering.
Swimming is complimentary for all in-house resident guests. Non-resident visitors are warmly welcome to enjoy our swimming facilities and iconic footbridge at KES 500 for adults and KES 300 for children per day. Fresh towels and changing room facilities are provided.
